TASK 11 – FINAL PLANT NETWORK DESIGN
Recommendation: fibre backbone + shielded copper at robot cells + Wi-Fi 6 for office/general mobility + private 5G URLLC for future AGVs/critical mobility.

Media:
- Robot cell near welders: Cat6A S/FTP for noise immunity and 100 m Ethernet reach.
- 140 m office/shop-floor and 550 m shop-floor/data-centre runs: fibre.
- 400 km head-office path: carrier/leased single-mode fibre service.
Wireless:
- Office/user devices: Wi-Fi 6.
- AGVs/strict mobile control: private 5G URLLC.
- High-rate CCTV: wired Ethernet/fibre/eMBB where mobility is required.
Error/flow control:
- Ethernet CRC for link errors.
- TCP/ARQ for ordinary IP traffic.
- FEC is preferred for latency-sensitive noisy wireless links.
- Sliding-window protocols are needed on the 400 km path; stop-and-wait gives only about 0.299% utilisation on the 1 Gbps, 1500-byte frame example.
Key numbers from earlier tasks: one line is 158.24 Mbps; eight lines are 1265.92 Mbps, so a single 1 Gbps uplink is insufficient. Local CCTV recording reduces a line to 67.04 Mbps, making 1 Gbps sufficient for one line but not necessarily the eight-line plant aggregate.
Risks: RF interference/roaming variability for wireless control, and WAN latency/capacity dependence on the carrier.
Five-plant scale: use redundant fibre/core switching and adequate inter-plant WAN capacity; do not aggregate five plants onto an undersized 1 Gbps link.
Alternative all-private-5G design: use 5G for AGVs, mobile control and selected cameras; keep fixed high-volume CCTV/backbone traffic on wired fibre/copper for predictable capacity and latency.
