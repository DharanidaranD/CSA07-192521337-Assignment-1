TASK 4 ANSWER
(a) Robot cell, 85 m near welders: Cat6A S/FTP — shielding and 100 m Ethernet limit.
(b) Office to shop floor, 140 m: fibre is preferred because it exceeds the 100 m copper channel limit; use multimode fibre for a campus/plant run.
(c) Shop floor to data centre, 550 m: multimode fibre.
(d) Plant to head office, 400 km: single-mode fibre via leased/carrier service; ordinary copper Ethernet cannot span 400 km.
Shannon at 40 dB SNR: 3321.96 Mbps. At 15 dB: 1256.95 Mbps. Lower SNR reduces theoretical capacity significantly and indicates a noisier robot environment.
Wi-Fi 6 at 160 MHz and 15 dB: 804.45 Mbps theoretical Shannon limit; wireless still has variable interference, contention, jitter and handover risk, so it is not preferred for 10 ms robot control.
Measured throughput is below Shannon because Shannon is an ideal upper bound; protocol overhead, coding, implementation limits, interference and contention reduce practical throughput.
