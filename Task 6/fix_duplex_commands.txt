Linux:
sudo ethtool -s eth0 speed 100 duplex full autoneg on
Verify:
ethtool eth0

Preferred: enable auto-negotiation at both ends. If forced, configure both ends identically.

Windows PowerShell (example; adapter name may differ):
Get-NetAdapter
Set-NetAdapterAdvancedProperty -Name "Ethernet" -DisplayName "Speed & Duplex" -DisplayValue "Auto Negotiation"
